# Samples

One clip per engine, each made by one request to the same server. Under every player is the text
that request sent, cue tags included, so you can hear what each tag did and whether the engine
performed it.

The cloned voices are Sonny and Tiffani, two hosts from [deadair.radio](https://deadair.radio).
Both were invented with a voice-design tool rather than recorded from anybody, and each engine that
clones learned them from the same reference clip: 7 to 8 seconds of it, with its transcript, for
Dia, Fish and Breeze, and the first 3.6 seconds alone for StyleTTS 2. Kokoro and Orpheus cannot clone,
so they speak in one of their own voices.

Every clip came from rhapsode 0.1.26 in Docker on an RTX 4070 Ti SUPER, in October 2026. Each line
was generated with three seeds and the best take kept. The takes left out misread "rhapsode", stopped
early, or repeated words from the reference clip. The Fish and Breeze weights are licensed for
research and not for commercial use, which [Engines](engines/index.md) explains.
