Chatterbox now declares `segmentation.segmentCharacters: 300`, so the SDK speaks long text as several generations joined into one response. Every Chatterbox build stops at 40 seconds of speech per generation, and a 1,060-character news bulletin handed to turbo whole came back as 26.7 seconds of skipped and crammed speech. `protocol.md` § 8 says where the number came from.
