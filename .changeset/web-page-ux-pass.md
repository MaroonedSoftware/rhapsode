The web page reads better and asks before the costly things:

- A list that is still loading or failed to load now says so, rather than that it is empty.
- Job progress is announced to screen readers, and a job the page just started is scrolled to and focused.
- Each page has its own tab title, and the nav marks the current page.
- Engines, jobs and keep-alive settings go by their display names, dials read as words ("CFG weight"), and a failed job names its step as the stepper does.
- Reinstall all and Reset now ask first. Leaving Settings with an unsaved change asks too, and a generated token can be copied before it is saved.
- The job log follows new lines only while you are at the bottom, with a button to jump back, and a failure shows its last line above the log.
- An installed engine's card links to Try it. The expiry countdown ticks every second, a voice preview can be stopped from its row, and Cmd or Ctrl and Enter speaks the line.
