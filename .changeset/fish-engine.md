Fish Audio S2 Pro is in the catalog, built with Fish Audio: the model that ranked second among open weights on the Artificial Analysis speech arena at its release. It speaks ten languages, performs `laugh`, `chuckle` and `clear throat`, and clones from a clip and its `transcript`. **Its code and its weights are for research and non-commercial use only**, under the Fish Audio Research License, so an install has to accept `Fish-Audio-Research-License`. rhapsode ships none of its code: the engine downloads Fish Audio's inference code from upstream at a pinned commit, beside the weights, and checks it. It fits a 16 GB NVIDIA card, at 12.3 GiB. `rhapsode-engine-fish` is the new adapter package.
