StyleTTS 2 is in the catalog: Yinghao Aaron Li's style-diffusion model, which clones a voice from three seconds of audio with no transcript. It is 191M parameters in 1.2 GiB, several times realtime on a laptop's CPU and faster on its GPU, speaks English only and performs no cues. It has four dials from upstream's own inference: `alpha` and `beta` for how much of the timbre and the prosody come from the text rather than the clip, `diffusionSteps`, and `embeddingScale`. Six stock voices are speakers from LibriTTS and LibriSpeech, the corpora it was trained on. Its code is MIT and phonemizes through GPL code, so what the worker runs is GPL-3.0-or-later; its weights are MIT, and upstream asks that listeners be told the speech is synthesised unless the person cloned has agreed. `rhapsode-engine-styletts2` is the new adapter package; like Fish, it downloads upstream's code at a pinned commit beside the weights, and checks every checkpoint against a pinned digest before torch unpickles it.
